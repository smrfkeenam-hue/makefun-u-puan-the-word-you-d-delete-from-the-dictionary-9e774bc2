# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun bounty "The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary"
(`u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2`).

- `main.py` (stdlib only) writes exactly one word to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`: **irregardless** — no explanation,
  no trailing newline.
- The pick: the dictionary's most famous non-word. "Regardless" wearing a
  redundant prefix; deleting it is the dictionary correcting its own
  mistake, a joke that lands the moment the word is gone.
- `README.md` carries the three live make.fun badges. `LICENSE` is MIT.
